A local institution with global ambition

Shielding the Curious. A safe space to learn, build and belong.

Mission

To democratize access to data science and technology education by creating inclusive, community-driven learning environments where knowledge is shared peer-to-peer.

Model

We combine rigorous instruction, peer accountability, and intensive project work to accelerate competence, confidence, and employability.

Why It Matters

When local talent gains technical agency, communities gain stronger institutions, better services, and innovation rooted in real needs.

Global Partnership with DataCamp

Flagship Program: Foundations of Data Science and AI

A streamlined 10-week journey from core skills to venture-ready capstone delivery.

Weeks 1-5

Phase 1: Core Foundations

  • Data and AI fundamentals
  • Python for applied data work
  • Data cleaning and feature engineering
  • Supervised and unsupervised learning
Weeks 6-9

Phase 2: Applied AI

  • Neural networks and deep learning
  • Generative AI foundations
  • Applied AI in real-world contexts
  • Responsible AI and ethics
Week 10

Phase 3: Capstone and Selection

Teams present to reviewers and top projects advance to the Ngao Labs Incubation Programme.
