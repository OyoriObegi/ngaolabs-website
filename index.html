Building ethical data and AI leaders for Africa.

Ngao Labs is a registered Public Benefit Organization (PBO) in Kenya dedicated to expanding access to world-class technology education. We deliver high-quality, tuition-free training in data science, artificial intelligence, software development and other emerging digital technologies, empowering talented individuals from underserved communities to build meaningful careers and solve real-world problems.

Mission

To democratize access to data science and technology education by creating inclusive, community-driven learning environments where knowledge is shared peer-to-peer.

Model

We combine rigorous instruction, peer accountability, and intensive project work to accelerate competence, confidence, and employability.

Why It Matters

When local talent gains technical agency, communities gain stronger institutions, better services, and innovation rooted in real needs.

Flagship Program: Foundations of Data Science and AI

A streamlined 10-week journey from core skills to venture-ready capstone delivery.

Weeks 1-5

Phase 1: Core Foundations

  • Data and AI fundamentals
  • Python for Data Science
  • Data cleaning and feature engineering
  • Supervised and unsupervised learning
Weeks 6-9

Phase 2: Applied AI

  • Neural networks and deep learning
  • Generative AI foundations
  • Applied AI in real-world contexts
  • Responsible AI and ethics
Week 10

Phase 3: Capstone and Selection

Teams present to reviewers and top projects advance to the Ngao Labs Incubation Programme.

Why Choose Ngao Labs

More than just a bootcamp—an ecosystem designed for your success.

World-Class Tools, Zero Tuition

We believe financial barriers shouldn't limit potential. Enjoy a 100% tuition-free, 10-week intensive program with free access to premium global platforms like DataCamp, ensuring you train on the exact tools trusted by top tech companies.

Guided by Active Practitioners

Forget pre-recorded lectures. Our curriculum is delivered collaboratively by active industry professionals. From daily morning standups to intensive live sessions, you are guided by mentors who bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world tech.

Build Real Solutions

We don't just issue certificates; we launch products. Our program culminates in a rigorous capstone project evaluated by an external industry panel. Top teams advance into our Incubation Programme to turn ideas into viable market solutions.

A Safe Space to Belong

Tech can be intimidating, but you don't have to navigate it alone. We foster a culture of peer-to-peer accountability, psychological safety, and radical support. Here, we shield the curious so you can focus on building your future.
